E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and customer behavior data to understand revenue performance, customer retention, product category performance, purchasing patterns, and customer experience.

The project combines MySQL for data preparation and analysis with Power BI for interactive business intelligence and dashboard visualization.

The goal is to transform raw transaction data into clear, actionable insights that can support business decisions around customer retention, product performance, sales channels, and customer experience.

Dataset at a Glance

* 17,049 orders

* Business Questions

This analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:

* What is the overall revenue and sales performance?
* Which product categories generate the most revenue?
* How important are returning customers to total revenue?
* Which device types generate the most orders and revenue?
* Which payment methods are most commonly used?
* Which cities contribute the most revenue?
* What is the average customer rating and delivery time?
* How does sales performance change over time?

* Key Business Insights

1. Returning Customers Drive Revenue

Returning customers generated ₦19.19M, representing approximately 88.1% of total revenue. This indicates that repeat purchasing is a major contributor to revenue in the dataset.

2. Electronics Is the Leading Revenue Category

Electronics generated ₦10.48M, approximately 48% of total revenue, making it the largest revenue-generating product category.

3. Mobile Is the Leading Device Type

Mobile generated ₦12.03M from 9,543 orders, making it the leading device type by both revenue and order volume.

4. Credit Cards Generate the Most Revenue

Credit card transactions generated approximately ₦9.07M, the highest revenue among the payment methods analyzed.

5. Istanbul Leads City Revenue

Istanbul generated approximately ₦5.65M, followed by Ankara at ₦3.05M and Izmir at ₦2.65M.

6. Customer Experience Provides a Baseline for Improvement

The average customer rating was 3.90/5, while average delivery time was 6.5 days. These metrics provide useful baseline indicators for monitoring customer experience.

Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could be considered:

* Strengthen customer retention: Develop loyalty programs and personalized offers to encourage repeat purchases.
* Focus on high-revenue categories: Monitor Electronics and Home & Garden performance and identify opportunities for cross-selling and upselling.
* Optimize the mobile experience: Since mobile generates the highest order volume and revenue, prioritize mobile shopping performance and usability.
* Monitor customer experience: Track customer ratings and delivery times regularly to identify areas that may affect customer satisfaction.
* Investigate geographic opportunities: Analyze high-revenue cities in greater detail to understand demand patterns and potential expansion opportunities.
